Introduction

Welcome to FoodBite, a complete multi-restaurant food delivery and management system built using Node.js, MongoDB, Angular, and Flutter.

FoodBite is designed for startups, restaurants, and enterprises to launch and scale their food ordering business with ease 🚀

📌 What is FoodBite?

FoodBite is an all-in-one food ordering ecosystem that connects:

  • 👨‍🍳 Restaurants & Cloud Kitchens
  • 🧑‍💻 Admins
  • 🚴 Delivery Partners
  • 🍽️ Kitchen Staff & Waiters
  • 📱 Customers

The platform supports food delivery, self-pickup, dine-in, POS orders, table orders, and tiffin / meal subscriptions.

🧱 System Architecture

FoodBite follows a modular, API-first architecture, ensuring high performance, security, and scalability.

🛠️ Technology Stack

🔙 Backend

  • ⚙️ Node.js
  • 🚀 Express.js
  • 🗄️ MongoDB (Mongoose ODM)
  • 🔐 JWT Authentication
  • 🔁 RESTful APIs

🖥️ Admin Panel

  • 🅰️ Angular
  • 🎨 Angular Material
  • 🛡️ Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

📱 Mobile Apps

  • 💙 Flutter (Single Codebase)
  • 🤖 Android
  • 🍎 iOS
